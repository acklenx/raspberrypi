Build a Station

Pick what you want to measure. Each one is a sensor you wire to your Pico, with a picture of exactly how, and a one-click link that loads the code. Every station shows its reading on the little screen and on your phone.

The easy button: load everything at once

Not sure where to start, or want it all on one board? This single install puts every project below onto the Pico, each in its own folder. Stop one, open another, run it — no reloading.

Heat & air

Air conditions

measures temperature, humidity & pressure — sensor: BME280

One small part reads all three at once. Great for "inside the bin vs. outside" comparisons.

Install · Wiring

Waterproof temperature probes

measures temperature in soil or water — sensor: DS18B20

Rugged probes on a wire. Many can share one wire, so you can map temperature across a whole bin.

Install · Wiring

Water

Soil moisture

measures how wet the bedding is — sensor: capacitive moisture probe

Stick it in the soil; it reads dry-to-soaked as a percent. Calibrate in two minutes (dry in air, dip in water).

Install · Wiring

Light

Light, the simple way

measures brighter/darker — sensor: GL5528 photoresistor

A cheap classic. Perfect for "how dark is it inside the worm bin with the lid on?"

Install · Wiring

Light, the precise way

measures real light levels (lux) — sensor: BH1750

Actual numbers you can compare between rooms and days. (A shared classroom sensor.)

Install · Wiring

Distance & compaction

Soil compaction

measures the gap down to the bedding surface — sensor: VL53L0X laser

Mounted in the lid, it watches the surface. As bedding settles, the gap grows; a sudden jump means someone disturbed the bin.

Install · Wiring

Longer-range version

measures distance up to 4 meters — sensor: VL53L1X laser

For deeper bins with more headroom, or room-scale experiments.

Install · Wiring

Sound

Sound level

measures how loud it is — sensor: MAX9814 microphone

A live loudness meter. Clap, talk, or play music and watch the bar jump.

Install · Wiring

Make something move

Servo (a controllable arm)

an actuator: it moves to any angle you tell it

Drive it from a slider on your phone, sweep it automatically, or make it follow the light. The moment code moves something real.

Install · Wiring

The big one: the worm-bin monitor

Every sensor and actuator, on one Pico

The showcase build: two air sensors (inside & outside), a row of temperature probes, two moisture probes, light, sound, compaction, two servos, and a relay — all running together, with a full control dashboard. This is where a real worm-bin station lives.

Wiring pictures

Exactly how each one connects. Red = power, black = ground, green/white = the two shared "talking" wires, yellow = the sensor's own signal. Numbers outside the board are the physical pins you count to.
