Research Projects

Real questions you can answer with the gear in the kit. Most are a good fit for a high-school freshman working over a few class periods; a handful are marquee builds for a team that wants to go big. Every card says what it needs and flags when you'll have to trade gear with another group to pull it off.

How to read a card

Starter — one sensor, one session, clear result Solid — a real experiment with a control Stretch — multi-day or multi-sensor, more moving parts Mack Daddy — marquee build, show it off (only 5 of these) Meta — improve our own setup

Horse-trade flag: the orange box means the project needs more of something than one bin normally gets (extra temp probes, the shared geophones, a spare Pico), so you'll be borrowing or swapping with another group. Plan it with them and your teacher.
